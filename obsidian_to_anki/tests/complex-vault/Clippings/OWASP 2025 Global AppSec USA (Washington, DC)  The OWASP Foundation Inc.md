**Training Dates - November 3-5, 2025**

**Conference Dates - November 6-7, 2025**

  

**Get ready for the ultimate cybersecurity experience at the OWASP Global AppSec US Conference in Washington, D.C.!**

  

**Join Us for the Ultimate Application Security Experience - OWASP Global AppSec US 2025 in Washington, D.C.!**

  

From **November 6–7, 2025**, connect with over 800 hundred security experts, thought leaders, and practitioners. Be a part of something extraordinary at the **OWASP Global AppSec US Conference**, OWASP's largest US Conference, taking place in the heart of Washington, D.C. This is your opportunity to immerse yourself in the leading event for application security professionals, where passion, innovation, and community converge.

  

Ours is an event designed to **fuel** your passion for security. This is your chance to connect, learn, and grow with some of the brightest minds in the field. Be inspired by our keynote speakers and dive deep into six expertly curated tracks—including builder/developer, breaker, defender, manager-culture, and OWASP Projects. You'll find actionable insights, hands-on learning, and **the learned experience of** world-class keynote speakers.

  

Ask questions. Exchange ideas. Challenge perspectives. Our speaker sessions are designed to be interactive, thought-provoking, and deeply relevant to your work. Whether you're looking to sharpen your skills, explore emerging trends, or simply engage with your peers, this event is where growth happens.

  

**Love swag? We do too.** And it is much better when you can explore the cutting-edge exhibitor hall, fill up your vendor passport and get prizes at the raffle! And yes, you'll be scanned (if you choose to). But our vendor partners are nice, and you'll only get the information you actually need.

  

Challenge yourself in the thrilling **Capture the Flag** competition, get hands-on in the **OWASP Projects Demo Room**, and take part in the exclusive **Meet the Mentor** Program. Plus, with countless opportunities to network at receptions throughout the event, you'll make invaluable connections that could shape your career. Did we mention the nice vendors have parties? Yes! Parties! Where you can network. Or discuss that presentation. Or meet kindred spirits and embark on your next professional journey, solving problems in security!

  

Ready to go deeper? Don't miss our **hands-on training courses** from **November 3–5**, led by leading experts. It's the perfect way to enhance your knowledge and prepare for the main event. You will learn about threat modeling, AI security and security with AI, privacy and security programs from some of the best people out there - trainers carefully selected for the quality of their training, the depth of their material and their experience in the field!

  

We are at the center of innovation, learning, sharing and community - coming together to protect all the things! Mark your calendar now, and get ready for an experience you won't forget!

  

  

**\*Training and conference are two separate ticket purchases.**
